01 / The studio

Built from curiosity.
Driven by the love of games.

CosmicFire Studios began with a simple ambition: turn imagined worlds into something people can play. Founded by Maurice B. in 2021, the studio brings together a background in game design, a passion for art, and an ongoing commitment to software development.

We’re an independent studio at the beginning of our journey. Our work starts with ideas, grows through experimentation, and develops one prototype at a time. We believe memorable games begin with curiosity and the determination to keep creating.

Maurice B. Founder, CosmicFire Studios
